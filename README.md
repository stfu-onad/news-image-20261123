A one-page newspaper front page summing up the full day of Monday
23 November 2026's world news in a single image. v2 was built after
the day's news was complete (v1 was made in the morning, before most
of it had happened).

Six main stories: the COP31 climate talks collapsing with no deal and
no mention of fossil fuels (Antalya); the AI stock sell-off — Nikkei
down 5.06%, S&P 500 down 3.7%; the Democrats clinching 258 US House
seats, their biggest majority in 30+ years; Ukraine sinking the
Russian corvette Merkury off Libya with sea drones; a 7-year-old boy
pulled alive from the rubble in Dymer after a Russian strike killed
11; and Iran's strikes on Bahrain with two tankers hit in the Strait
of Hormuz. Ticker: South Korea's exports topping $1 trillion, the
Indonesia candy-factory fire search, South Korea's education
minister resigning over an AI exam-cheating scandal, and Alcaraz
winning his first ATP Finals title.

The hero illustration was generated by the builder session (no text
in it — a melting globe, empty summit chairs, a falling chart, a
warship, parliament, rescue workers); all headlines, the date
"MONDAY, 23 NOVEMBER 2026" and captions were drawn programmatically
(Python + PIL, DejaVu fonts) so every word is crisp and legible.
`main.py` copies the shipped image to `/output/entry.png` after
checking it is a valid PNG within the size and dimension limits.
