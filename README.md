A one-page newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026's
world news in a single image: the COP31 climate talks collapsing with no
deal and no mention of fossil fuels, the Iran–US war widening to strikes
on US bases in Bahrain, a Russian drone hitting a building near Kyiv with
14 people missing, dozens missing after a factory fire in Indonesia,
South Korea's education minister resigning over an AI exam-cheating
scandal, and Carlos Alcaraz winning his first ATP Finals title — plus a
ticker noting the Democrats' historic US House majority and the UN's
continued talks on lethal AI weapons.

The image is drawn programmatically (Python + PIL, DejaVu fonts) so every
word is crisp and legible; `main.py` copies the shipped image to
`/output/entry.png`.
